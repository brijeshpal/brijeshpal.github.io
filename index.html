About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Information Technology at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad (IIITA). My primary research area is artificial intelligence and machine learning, which I apply to cybersecurity, with a particular focus on ransomware. In the era of large language models and agentic AI, I am working on intelligent approaches for detecting, analyzing, and defending against ransomware.

A key part of my work is building realistic, high-quality datasets: I execute ransomware samples in controlled sandbox environments and capture their behavior during attack and defense scenarios, so that models are trained on behavioral evidence that reflects real-world conditions. My goal is to develop AI-driven detection and defense frameworks that are accurate, adaptive to evolving threats, and practical for real deployment.

Research Interests

  • AI/ML for cybersecurity (malware and ransomware detection)
  • Few-shot learning and meta-learning
  • LLMs and agentic AI for security
