About

I am a PhD scholar in the Department of Information Technology at the Indian Institute of Information Technology Allahabad (IIITA), supervised by Prof. S. Venkatesan and Prof. Krishna Pratap Singh. My primary research area is artificial intelligence and machine learning, which I apply to cybersecurity, with a particular focus on ransomware. In the era of large language models and agentic AI, I am working on intelligent approaches for detecting, analyzing, and defending against ransomware.

A key part of my work is building realistic, high-quality datasets: I execute ransomware samples in controlled sandbox environments and capture their behavior during attack and defense scenarios, so that models are trained on behavioral evidence that reflects real-world conditions. My goal is to develop AI-driven detection and defense frameworks that are accurate, adaptive to evolving threats, and practical for real deployment.

Research Interests

  • AI/ML with Cybersecurity — learning-based detection of malware, ransomware, and evolving threats.
  • Few-Shot & Meta-Learning — recognising unseen families from a handful of samples via variational and prototypical meta-learning.
  • Agentic AI & LLMs for Security — reasoning over API-call sequences, binaries, and program behaviour to aid defenders.
  • Machine & Deep Learning — representation learning, gradient-boosted models, and fair, robust deep networks.
